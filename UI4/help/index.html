What is StepBy?

StepBy is a community map of tactile paving, kerb ramps, audible signals and other things that make a route walkable. Everything on it was added by someone walking the route.

How do I record a route?

Open the map, allow location access, then press Start recording. Walk your route and press Stop when you are done. Your path is saved so others can see which streets have been surveyed.

What should I report?

Anything a person would want to know before walking somewhere: tactile paving, ramps, handrails, lifts, audible crossings, and also obstacles or places to take care.

Who can see what I add?

Reports and recorded routes are public. Your display name is shown next to them. Your exact location is never shared outside the points you choose to publish.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. Guest mode lets you browse the map and read reports. Signing in is only needed to add something.
